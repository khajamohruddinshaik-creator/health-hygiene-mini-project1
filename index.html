Healthy Food Guide

Eat a balanced diet every day to boost your immunity and energy.

Whole Grains

Brown rice, oats, and whole wheat give long-lasting energy throughout the day.

Proteins

Clean Water

Drink 7–8 glasses of safe, clean water daily to stay hydrated and flush out toxins.

Health Tips: Proper Hand Washing

Follow these 6 simple steps to keep your hands clean and germ-free.

Step 1: Wet Your Hands

Wet your hands with clean running water.

Step 2: Apply Soap

Apply enough soap to cover your hands.

Step 3: Rub Your Hands

Rub palms, fingers and the back of your hands.

Step 4: Clean Your Nails

Clean between your fingers and under your nails.

Step 5: Rinse Your Hands

Wash away the soap using clean water.

Step 6: Dry Your Hands

Dry your hands with a clean towel.
